Privacy Policy for Blackbird

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Blackbird is a personal desktop app that runs on its owner's own computer.

What it accesses

If the owner signs in with Google, Blackbird gets read-only access to Gmail and Google Calendar (the gmail.readonly and calendar.readonly scopes). It uses this to show unread email and upcoming events in the owner's briefing.

What it stores

Sign-in tokens are saved only on the owner's computer. Email and calendar content is read when needed and is not copied to any server.

What it shares

Nothing. Blackbird has no server and does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone. Blackbird's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Control

The owner can remove an account in the app, or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
